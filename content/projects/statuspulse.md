## What StatusPulse Does

**Beautiful Status Pages** - Custom domains, brand colors, historical uptime graphs.

**Uptime Monitoring** - HTTP/HTTPS endpoint monitoring, 30-second check intervals.

**Incident Management** - One-click incident creation, pre-built templates.

**Subscriber Notifications** - Email notifications, webhook integrations.

## Tech Stack

- FastHTML for the backend
- PostgreSQL for data
- Deployed on Fly.io
